<objective>
Per-tab and per-page residual cleanup: replace the loose `p-6` / `space-y-6` / `gap-6` page-wrapper paddings that survive the primitive + chrome passes, so every page reaches Linear-density baseline.

Purpose:
- Plans 02 (primitives) and 03 (chrome) tighten the highest-leverage surfaces. After those land, the remaining loose space is concentrated in page-level wrapper divs that hardcode `p-6` (24px) and `space-y-6` (24px gap). This plan is the surgical cleanup of those wrappers.
- Sweep order is intentional (CONTEXT.md "Sweep order suggestion"): tokens → primitives → chrome → per-tab. Each layer's density cascades down so this plan does NOT touch primitive consumers (tables, dialogs, buttons, inputs) — they already pick up the new defaults.
- **Hard exclusions (CONTEXT.md):**
  - **WBS internals** — `components/WbsGrid.tsx`, `components/WbsPageClient.tsx` are spec-tight from Phase 85. ONLY the `app/customer/[id]/wbs/page.tsx` wrapper changes.
  - **Gantt internals** — `components/GanttChart.tsx` is carefully tuned. ONLY the `app/customer/[id]/gantt/page.tsx` wrapper changes.
- The sweep also applies to global pages (`/`, `/daily-prep`, `/weekly-report`, `/knowledge-base`, `/outputs`, `/scheduler`, `/time-tracking`, `/settings`, `/search`).

Output:
- Every customer tab page (~21 routes) and every global page (~9 routes) has its top-level wrapper at `p-4` (16px) instead of `p-6` (24px).
- Top-level `space-y-6` / `gap-6` patterns are replaced with `space-y-3` / `gap-3` where used as page-body section gap. (CONTEXT.md "p-4 sections, gap-3 stacks".)
- Skipped: any sub-component imports (WbsGrid, GanttChart) — wrappers only.
</objective>
