## Decisions Made

- Used explicit per-step `--spacing-{n}` approach (not root `--spacing` scale) — safer one-to-one mapping, auditable in DevTools, less blast radius if values ever change
- Fractional and odd Tailwind steps (1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 7, 9, 10, 11) intentionally NOT aliased per plan constraints — no density benefit, unnecessary maintenance surface
- Kata step 7 (32px) maps to `--spacing-8` and Kata step 8 (48px) maps to `--spacing-12` — intentional misalignment between 8-step Kata scale and Tailwind's 4px-multiple infinite scale
